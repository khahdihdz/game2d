Dark Fantasy Survivor RPG PNG assets generated for LibGDX.

- characters: player actions, 4 frames per sheet, 64x64
- enemies: enemy sheets, 4 frames per sheet, 64x64
- bosses: 4 frames per sheet, 128x128
- weapons and vfx: 6 frames per sheet, 64x64
- items: 4 frames per sheet, 32x32
- tiles/tileset_32.png: 8x8 atlas of 32x32 tiles
- ui/icons_32.png: 8x4 atlas of 32x32 icons

The Pages workflow runs this generator before the TeaVM build so generated PNGs are copied from core/assets into the browser bundle.
